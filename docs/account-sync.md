# Account sync scheduling

Account discovery, post downloads, and prediction have separate lifecycles. The account's `sync_enabled` preference is authoritative; `sync_task` identifies queued/running work only. The existing account update API still accepts `sync` and `filter`.

Future checks remain in `accounts.sync_next_at` until due. The task scheduler performs two bounded lookups that can use the existing `accounts_sync_due` index: null schedules first (immediately due), then `sync_next_at <= NOW()` in ascending order. Together they select at most 20 eligible accounts per iteration. The timestamp comparisons do not wrap the indexed column in a function, and the ordering matches the index. Pending/running tasks are excluded unless their running lease has expired; enqueueing rechecks each account under a lock. Future schedules create no pending tasks until their time arrives.

## Storage and observation

`007_table_create_accounts.sql` defines sync enablement, scheduling, lease and prediction fields directly. `019_account_sync.sql` creates `account_post_observations` and the scheduling indexes through the existing database initializer. New accounts default to sync disabled; no existing task pointers or saved posts are backfilled. Observation rows cascade when an account is deleted.

Observations are unique by `(account_uid, platform_post_id)`. They retain publication and first-seen times independently of download filters. Corrected upstream publication times update the identified event. Distinct posts sharing a timestamp remain separate records; prediction groups simultaneous timestamps into one event. The forecast reads at most the latest 1,000 observed posts, while older observations remain available.

A sync enumerates the complete platform listing, including pinned and older posts, recording observations before selecting downloads. Only successful completion advances `sync_checked_at` and the legacy `latest_update`. The timestamp is captured before enumeration, not after downloads or prediction, so listing duration is not falsely counted as confirmed silence. Partial listings retain their discovered events but do not advance the observation boundary. Parser submissions establish complete coverage when the listing finishes successfully and all posts remain selected. The frontend includes the listing start time as `posts_scanned_at` only for those submissions; subsets and submissions without that timestamp do not establish complete coverage.

Discovery queues independent `post_parse` jobs. Existing completed posts and pending/running downloads are skipped. Failed downloads can be retried through the existing task retry action, or rediscovered with fresh media URLs at a subsequent account check. A successful account sync means discovery completed; download outcomes are separate and appear in Sync details.

## Prediction

The predictor compares a conjugate Gamma/exponential baseline with regularized one-, two-, and three-component log-normal renewal candidates. Renewal intensity includes UTC daily and weekly harmonics, bounded recent activity, and a four-knot Gaussian random-walk log-activity effect. Beyond the observation boundary, activity reverts toward baseline over 90 days with simulated process uncertainty.

Same-platform account median log intervals provide an empirical population prior when available. Each peer contributes one median, with at most 30 peers and 1,000 timestamps per peer. The population cutoff precedes the held-out suffix. Otherwise the explicit default prior has an expected posting rate of one event per 24 hours and Gamma shape 2.

With fewer than five distinct events only the conjugate model is eligible. Otherwise candidates train on the first 80% (at least three events), are scored on the chronological suffix, and are refitted with all events and current right-censored silence. Mixture weights are the softmax of mean held-out log densities. They are predictive score weights, not exact Bayesian model probabilities or fitted stacking weights.

Renewal parameter inference uses MAP with analytic objective gradients and a full Hessian obtained by central differences of those gradients, with 64 reproducible bounded parameter draws for the Laplace approximation. This reduces curvature evaluation from quadratically many scalar losses to twice the parameter count in gradient evaluations. Nonconvergent fits or non-positive posterior curvature exclude that candidate with a visible warning. An exponential baseline remains available; numerical failures are not silently reported as a successful complex fit. Prediction runs in a worker thread, outside the async request loop. A bounded, 32-entry process-local cache reuses fitted distributions only when posting history, the verified observation boundary, and population prior inputs match. Current-time scheduling bounds are recalculated on every request.

Forecasts average conditional survival curves and expose median time, an approximate central 90% interval, and probabilities within 1, 6, 24, and 168 hours of the last verified observation. They also store 97 samples of the cumulative probability curve for display, spaced uniformly in log(elapsed hours), starting at eight hours. The plot extends to at least 24 hours, then to the 95th percentile or the 366-day horizon without renormalizing either the first eight hours or the remaining tail. Quantiles beyond the 366-day numerical horizon are null, not clamped into false certainty. This is approximate inference; forecast calibration and accuracy have not been established against production histories.

## Scheduling and recovery

The policy in `utils/sync_prediction.py` checks at 63% predicted probability of at least one new post, bounded to 13 minutes–30 days from calculation. Completion applies the same bounds relative to completion time. If the target time is already past, the minimum interval applies. An empty successful check conditions on the extended silence without resetting the last publication time. Predictions are recomputed from history, so repeated checks do not count silence twice. Existing saved schedules are replaced after the next sync; enabling sync without a saved schedule and manual checks can start immediately.

Errors use a separate exponential retry delay starting at 13 minutes and capped at six hours (13, 26, 52, 104, 208, then 360 minutes). They do not advance the observation boundary. Disabling automatic sync removes pending automatic work and prevents a running job from scheduling its successor. Manual checks are still allowed and deduplicate with pending/running discovery. Full and ordinary checks both enumerate the listing and skip completed downloads.

Discovery ownership has a two-minute database lease renewed every 30 seconds. Superseded workers are fenced before observation writes. An account discovery is bounded to one hour. Expired discovery jobs are marked failed and replaced when due. Download workers also renew liveness; expired download work is marked failed and enabled accounts are scheduled for rediscovery. The local queue limits discovery-generated downloads to two active workers, reserving capacity for account checks, and gives due account checks priority.

Account creation saves the profile and downloads matching posts inside the account task; parser submissions download their selected posts there as well. Post progress stays nested under the account or creator task, which waits for the downloads and reports failures. These initial downloads do not create separate post jobs. After successful initial downloads, complete parser scans record the observation boundary and calculate the next check from the submitted posts, reusing the submitted profile without an immediate refetch. Automatic sync is configured after successful initial downloads; subsequent syncs retain the independent discovery and download lifecycle described above.

## Details

`GET /api/account/sync/details?uid=...` returns persisted status and a freshly calculated forecast from stored posting history. It does not fetch upstream data, advance the last verified observation, or change the saved schedule. **Sync details** in the account's overflow menu refreshes every 15 seconds while open. A headerless table displays observation coverage, the actual saved next check, failures, and download counts. One median forecast and its cumulative probability plot share a single panel; scheduling explanations and model warnings are collapsed. Accounts with a verified observation can display the plot immediately, even when their older saved forecast lacks curve data. Times are displayed in the browser's locale; forecast calendar features use UTC. **Edit Sync Settings** saves automatic sync enablement and download filters through the existing account update API.
